Day Detail — Free Time (Pacific)

How free time is computed: each game blocks Pacific time from its start for its estimated average duration (MLB 158m, NFL 192m, CFB 200m, MLS 120m — football includes halftime). Free time is the complementary window(s). Times are PT (Aug–Oct = PDT, UTC−7). TBD-time games are shown but excluded from the estimate. Filter the leagues above to isolate any one team. Source data & official links: schedules.md (96 verified entries).
Key assumption: "no MLB games on" is interpreted as San Francisco Giants regular-season games (2026 MLB regular season ends Sep 27). See schedules.md FLAGS for review items.
