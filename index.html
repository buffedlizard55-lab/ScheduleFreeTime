When am I free?

A time counts as busy if any of these is on air: any MLB game (all 30 clubs), the San Francisco 49ers, the San Jose Earthquakes, Stanford football, or Cal football. Everything is shown in America/Los_Angeles for Aug 1 – Oct 31, 2026.
