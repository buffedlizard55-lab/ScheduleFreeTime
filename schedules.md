## Free-time calculation method

- For each day, every game is treated as a BUSY block from its PT start time for its estimated average duration.
- Free time = the complement of all busy blocks across the 24-hour day (12:00 AM - 11:59 PM).
- Durations (research-based averages): MLB 158 min, NFL 192 min, CFB 200 min, MLS 120 min. Football durations include halftime.
- Games with TBD times are shown but excluded from the busy-block estimate until the time is confirmed.
- Doubleheaders (Giants vs Diamondbacks, Aug 29) create two busy blocks with a free window between them.
